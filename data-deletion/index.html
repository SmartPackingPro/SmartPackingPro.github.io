Privacy & data

Delete your data

Remove local trip and Travel Vault data, delete copies you exported yourself, and request help with any applicable off-device records.

There are no user accounts

The current Smart Packing Pro app does not provide user accounts. There is no account profile to close and no account-level data deletion process. Your trips, packing lists, settings, and Travel Vault documents are stored on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Developer: Smart Packing Pro · devlopersamartha@gmail.com

1. Delete local app data

On Android, open Settings → Apps → Smart Packing Pro → Storage → Clear Data, or uninstall the app.

This removes the local copies of your trips, packing lists, itineraries, templates, reminders, settings, and Travel Vault documents. Clear or uninstall only after exporting anything you need to keep; the developer has no server copy from which to restore deleted local data.

2. Remove exported copies

Encrypted backup ZIP files and exported or shared trip codes are separate copies that you control. Delete them from every place where you saved or shared them, such as Downloads, a file manager, cloud storage, email, messages, or another device.

Smart Packing Pro cannot remove copies stored outside the app, and clearing the app does not remove those copies.

3. Request help with off-device data

To ask us to delete applicable diagnostics we hold, or to ask a privacy question, email devlopersamartha@gmail.com.

Please provide only information reasonably needed to process the request, such as the app version, an approximate date, and a short description of the feature or issue. Do not email Travel Vault files, passwords, backup archives, trip codes, passport scans, or other sensitive travel documents.

Limits on technical service data

Depending on the app feature and your choices, technical service data may be processed by providers such as GeoNames, MET Norway, GitHub, Google Firebase, or Google Play. The current privacy policy explains which services apply. These providers may receive standard network information and keep records under their own policies.

We cannot guarantee deletion from a provider's systems or promise a deletion deadline. We may also be unable to identify an individual diagnostics record from the information we receive. Provider records are subject to the provider's own identification, deletion, and retention limitations.
