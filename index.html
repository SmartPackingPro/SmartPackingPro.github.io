Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to help you travel smarter.

Do I need an internet connection to pack?

Smart Packing Pro is built privacy-first. Everything stays on your phone your trips, your lists, your documents. Only your city's name is ever sent out, just for weather and packing guides. No account, no tracking. And those guides? Over four hundred destinations, growing automatically no app update needed.

What is Trip Genius AI?

Trip Genius already covers over four hundred cities and countries and the list keeps growing. New destinations get added all the time, and they show up in your app automatically. No updates to download, no waiting. Just more places, ready when you are.

Can I create a custom itinerary for hidden gems?

Absolutely! Anyone can build a custom itinerary by hand every activity, every day even for hidden gems our AI doesn't cover yet. Then send the entire trip to a fellow traveler offline: scan a QR, paste a code, or beam it phone to phone. Real trips, from people who've walked the streets.

Is my data secure?

Built privacy-first. Your trips, your lists, your documents all stay on your phone. The only thing we ever send out is your destination's name, just to grab the weather and your packing guide. No account. No tracking. No personal data. Ever. We also use AES-256 encryption for the Travel Vault.

Is it really free?

Yes, you can start completely free. The free version gives you 5 lifetime trips and all core packing features with no subscription traps. We also offer a 7-day Pro trial if you want to test premium features.
